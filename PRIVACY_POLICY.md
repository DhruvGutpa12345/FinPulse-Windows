# Privacy Policy for FinPulse - XIRR Calculator

**Last Updated:** October 15, 2026  
**Developer:** Dhruv Gupta  
**Application:** FinPulse - XIRR Calculator (FinPulse Windows)

---

### 1. Introduction

Dhruv Gupta ("we", "our", or "developer") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use **FinPulse - XIRR Calculator** ("the Application"), a desktop financial calculator application available via the Microsoft Store and GitHub.

---

### 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

FinPulse is built on a **privacy-first, local-only architecture**. 

- **No Personal Data:** We do not collect, store, transmit, or solicit any personal identification information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address).
- **No Financial Data:** All financial figures, cash flows, loan balances, interest rates, investment amounts, and calculation results remain strictly on your local device. We have no access to your financial inputs.
- **No Telemetry or Tracking:** The Application contains no third-party tracking libraries, advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics, or telemetry monitoring services.
- **No Account Required:** You do not need to create an account, register, or provide credentials to use any feature of the Application.

---

### 3. Data Storage & Local Preferences

- **Application Preferences:** User settings (such as chosen visual theme, Windows 11 backdrop material, preferred currency notation, and default calculation parameters) are stored exclusively on your local computer in standard application data directories (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\FinPulse`).
- **CSV & Clipboard Exports:** When you choose to export cash flow schedules to CSV or copy calculation summaries to your clipboard, the output is saved directly to your local file system or Windows clipboard. No data is transmitted externally.
- **Diagnostics:** In the rare event of an unhandled runtime error, diagnostic crash logs are written strictly to your local system's temporary directory (`%TEMP%\FinPulse_Crash.log`). These files are never automatically uploaded to any remote server and can be cleared at any time from the in-app Settings menu.

---

### 4. Internet Connectivity & Permissions

FinPulse is an **offline desktop application**. It does not require an active internet connection to execute any mathematical calculation, model, or visualization. 

External web requests only occur if you explicitly click the optional link in the Settings page to visit the public open-source repository on GitHub (`https://github.com/DhruvGutpa12345/FinPulse-Windows`), which opens in your default system web browser subject to GitHub's privacy terms.

---

### 5. Third-Party Services

The Application does not partner with or use any third-party services that collect information about your identity or usage patterns.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy

The Application does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected in this document with a revised "Last Updated" date and published on our official open-source repository.
